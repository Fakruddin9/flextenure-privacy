You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law and necessary verification. 9. Children's Privacy

FlexTenure is not intended for children where use would be prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.
